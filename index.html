Our Program

We are building a six-bed supervised transitional living home in Trenton, New Jersey, for young adults ages 18–21 who are aging out of foster care. It will be a safe, stable place to live while they build the skills, income, and confidence they need for independent adult life.

Residents will receive:

  • Housing in a safe, supportive home
  • Education support, including help earning a GED
  • Employment support and job readiness
  • Financial literacy
  • Life skills
  • Transportation support
  • Case management
  • Support moving on to permanent housing

Young adults may stay in the program for up to 24 months, and no resident stays past age 21 — whichever comes first.

What We Ask of Residents

Our home works because everyone in it is working toward independence. Every resident is expected to:

  • Hold a job
  • Attend counseling
  • Obtain a GED if they do not already have one

How It Works

  1. Referral

    A young adult aging out of foster care is referred to Tender Teachings, and we meet to talk through the program, the expectations, and whether the home is the right fit.

  2. Moving In

    Once accepted, the resident moves into the six-bed home in Trenton and settles in with a plan — a job, counseling, and a path toward a GED if they do not already have one.

  3. Support While Living There

    While living in the home, residents get steady, hands-on support: education and employment help, financial literacy and life skills, transportation, and case management — all while building toward independence.

  4. Moving On to Permanent Housing

    Before their stay ends — at 24 months or by age 21 — we work with each resident on a plan for permanent housing, so they leave the program with a place to go and the skills to stay there.

Building Your Next Chapter Workshops

Our free life-skills workshops help young adults ages 18–21 get ready for work and independent living. The series runs for six Saturdays, 10:00 AM–1:00 PM, in Mercer County.

Across the six sessions, participants will work on:

  • Resume building
  • Job skills
  • CPR certification
  • Financial literacy
  • Books, materials, and class fees — provided as part of the program

These workshops are free for participants. They are made possible by the generosity of our donors and grant supporters.
